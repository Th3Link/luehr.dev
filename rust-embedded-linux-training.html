Who is it for?

Development teams using Rust for applications and services on Linux devices.

Prior knowledge

As an advanced training, this course builds on Rust Basics. We discuss your team’s existing knowledge and suitable preparation before the training.

What we cover

  • Iterators & declarative collection processing
  • Cargo features
  • Async with smol and tokio
  • Crate & dependency management
  • Synchronization
  • D-Bus
  • Web server with axum
  • Testing async code, especially tokio
  • Advanced CI/CD: audit, SBOM, Renovate
  • Security

How we work

Plenty of coding, with technical explanations where they are needed. The exercise program from Rust Basics grows with each new topic. We discuss prior knowledge and suitable topics before the training.

Frequently asked questions

Can the training be adapted?

Topic and technology requests are possible by prior agreement. Depending on prior knowledge, we spend less time on some topics and explore specific questions in depth.

When does the training take place?

Dates are arranged individually with your team.

Is the price per person?

No. It is a fixed price for a team of 5–10 participants, plus VAT. Travel is included in the on-site developer training price.
