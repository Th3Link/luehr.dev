02 / Approach

Write the code.
Understand the decisions.

In developer training, we solve concrete problems. Language concepts and technical explanations follow when they are needed.

A program that grows with you

In Rust Basics, we build a small game and extend it with each topic. The advanced trainings build on it. By arrangement, Basics can use a first program from your domain instead.

Connected to your work

Cross-compilation, tests, debugging and CI/CD are part of the work. We agree on topics and technologies beforehand. My goal is to help your team continue independently.

Want to evaluate Rust first? The managers workshop covers adoption, effort and decision criteria.
