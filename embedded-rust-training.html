Frequently asked questions

What prior knowledge do we need for Rust Basics?

Basic knowledge of any programming language. C is ideal, C++ works too, and we can also work well coming from Go or Python.

How much does a training cost?

Each training has a fixed price for a team of 5–10 participants: €12,000 remote, €15,000 on-site (4 days, Tuesday to Friday noon, travel costs included), each plus VAT.

In which language are the trainings held?

In English or German, whichever suits your team.

Can the content be adapted to our team?

Yes. Topic and technology requests are no problem when agreed beforehand. Depending on prior knowledge, some topics are slimmed down or specific wishes covered in depth.

Which hardware is used in the no_std training?

STM32, nRF52 or ESP32, with one platform chosen per training. Your custom hardware can be covered as well.

Can we work on our own project during the training?

In Rust Basics, by arrangement, we can build a first productive program from your domain instead of the exercise game.
