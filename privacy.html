Privacy Policy

General Information

This website can be used without actively providing personal data. Personal data is only collected if you contact me directly via email.

Local Storage and Cookies

This website does not use cookies.

To remember your theme preference (light or dark mode), a small piece of information is stored locally in your browser using the Local Storage API. This data is not transmitted to the server and is used solely to improve user experience.

Server Log Files

The hosting provider automatically collects and stores information in server log files. This may include:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referrer URL
  • Time of request
  • IP address

This data is used solely to ensure the proper operation and security of the website. It is not combined with any other data source. Log data is automatically deleted after a maximum of 7 days.

Contact

If you contact me via email, your data will be stored for the purpose of processing your request.

GitHub Statistics

This website embeds external content from GitHub (github-readme-stats). Your IP address may be transmitted to GitHub servers when loading this content.

External Resources

This website uses external resources (e.g. Lucide icons via unpkg CDN). When loading these resources, your IP address may be transmitted to the respective provider.

Scheduling Appointments via Zeeg

To book an introductory call, this website links to the scheduling service Zeeg (Zeeg GmbH, Friedrichstr. 155, 10117 Berlin, Germany). Simply visiting this website does not transmit any data to Zeeg. Only when you follow the link are you taken to Zeeg's website.

If you book an appointment there, the data you enter (e.g. name, email address, chosen date and optional message) is processed by Zeeg and transmitted to me so that I can prepare and hold the appointment. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract).

More information can be found in Zeeg's privacy policy: https://zeeg.me/en/legal/privacy

Liability Insurance Seal of exali AG

Description and scope of data processing: This website embeds the liability insurance seal of exali AG. The graphic element of the seal is loaded from the servers of exali AG. Due to the technical design of the internet, your IP address is processed in order to transmit the graphic to your browser.

If you click on the seal, you leave this website and are redirected to the servers of exali AG.

More information can be found in exali's privacy policy: exali.de privacy policy

Legal basis: The legal basis for the data processing is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).

Purpose: The data processing serves to provide the legally required information on professional liability insurance pursuant to § 2(11) DL-InfoV in a visually appealing way.

Legitimate interest: My legitimate interest results from the purpose of offering an appealing website and fulfilling my information obligations in a visually appealing manner.

Your Rights

You have the right to request information about your stored personal data, as well as correction or deletion of such data.
