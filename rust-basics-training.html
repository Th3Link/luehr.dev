What we cover

  • Toolchain
  • Types
  • Functions
  • Structs
  • Naming conventions
  • Ownership & borrowing
  • Conditions & loops
  • Enums (also with different member types)
  • match in many flavours
  • Error handling
  • Optional values
  • Code base structure
  • Visibility
  • Traits: derive, manual, your own
  • Unit & integration tests
  • Embedded cross compilation
  • Logging
  • Collections
  • Debugging
  • CI/CD basics: build, test, release

How we work

We develop a small game together. Each problem introduces the next language concept. You work individually, in small teams or as a group. By arrangement, we can build a first productive program from your domain instead.

Frequently asked questions

Can the training be adapted?

Topic and technology requests are possible by prior agreement. Depending on prior knowledge, we spend less time on some topics and explore specific questions in depth.

When does the training take place?

Dates are arranged individually with your team.

Is the price per person?

No. It is a fixed price for a team of 5–10 participants, plus VAT. Travel is included in the on-site developer training price.
