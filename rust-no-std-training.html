Who is it for?

Firmware and embedded developers who want to use Rust on microcontrollers.

Prior knowledge

As an advanced training, this course builds on Rust Basics. We discuss your team’s existing knowledge and suitable preparation before the training.

What we cover

  • Hardware setup
  • no_std vs. std
  • Cargo features
  • Async with Embassy
  • Embedded traits: I²C, SPI, CAN, …
  • Memory constraints
  • heapless
  • Custom allocator
  • Logging & debugging: defmt, SWD, JTAG
  • Iterators
  • Useful traits
  • Security

How we work

We work on one platform: STM32, nRF52 or ESP32. Your own custom hardware can be covered as well. The exercise program grows step by step with embedded topics. We agree on the platform and prior knowledge beforehand.

Frequently asked questions

Can the training be adapted?

Topic and technology requests are possible by prior agreement. Depending on prior knowledge, we spend less time on some topics and explore specific questions in depth.

When does the training take place?

Dates are arranged individually with your team.

Is the price per person?

No. It is a fixed price for a team of 5–10 participants, plus VAT. Travel is included in the on-site developer training price.
